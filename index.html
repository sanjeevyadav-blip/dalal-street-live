This table ranks stocks by data that's actually measurable — price, momentum, valuation — pulled the same way as everything else on this page. It deliberately has no "target sell price," "time to hold," or "probability of success" column: nobody can honestly compute a probability that a stock hits a price by a date, and a specific target price is a forecast dressed up as a fact. In India, publishing stock recommendations with buy/sell/target prices is also restricted to SEBI-registered Research Analysts and Investment Advisers. Use this as a starting point for your own research, not a tip sheet.

Prices and history come client-side from Yahoo Finance's public endpoints (via a CORS relay, since Yahoo blocks direct browser requests from other sites). Per Yahoo's own data-delay disclosures, NSE symbols (.NS) are treated as real-time and BSE (.BO) runs ~15 minutes behind — but these are unofficial, unsupported endpoints, so treat everything here as informational, not investment advice. Fundamentals (P/E, ROE, analyst targets, etc.) come from a separate, more fragile Yahoo endpoint that occasionally blocks unauthenticated requests — when that happens the panel says so and still shows price and technicals, which are computed locally from price history and don't depend on that endpoint. Recent news headlines are pulled live from Google News at the moment you open a stock, so they're never stale — but they're unfiltered search results, not vetted analysis, so read the source before acting on anything.
